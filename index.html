I'm Collins, a full-stack developer from Kenya working almost entirely in the JavaScript ecosystem. On GitHub I go by @CollOH-cOde, where I keep a growing collection of builds: storefronts, project platforms, a recipe site and yes, plenty of practice collections too.

My day-to-day is JS, TypeScript, React, Node.js and MongoDB, the classic MERN picture. I care about systems that actually hold up outside the demo: sane structure, honest error handling, pages that load fast on slow connections.

Who is Collins? +

A full-stack developer from Kenya building in the JavaScript ecosystem: JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Node.js, Express and MongoDB, with 12 public repositories on GitHub under @CollOH-cOde. I build websites, web apps and REST APIs, and I'm available for jobs, freelance work and remote contracts.

Why should you hire a developer? +

Because manual and repetitive work belongs in a system, not in your day. A website that wins customers, an app that handles bookings or inventory, an API that connects your tools. These keep working after you've gone to bed. Hiring a developer is investing in fewer errors and a business that doesn't stop when you do.

What does a digital presence actually mean? +

It's what people find before they ever contact you: a website that loads fast and says clearly what you do, profiles that are consistent, and proof of your work. Social media alone isn't a presence; your own space online is the baseline for trust.

How do we start a project together? +

Reach out through GitHub with what you're trying to build. We'll define the scope, the stack and the timeline, then I get to work with regular check-ins so you always know what's shipped and what's next. Small projects start small; nothing needs to be perfect at the start.
